The greyscale check, measured

Each glyph is rasterised at 16 px on a 2× grid, with colour discarded, and every pair is compared as ink: the share of marked pixels the two do not have in common. It is a floor, not a verdict — the gate is a human looking at the closest pairs, which is why they are named rather than counted.
