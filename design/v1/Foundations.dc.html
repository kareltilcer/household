Three surface steps, and no level three

Dark themes cannot rely on shadow, so every elevation level is also a surface step. A third level would have no dark surface to land on, which is the whole reason the ramp is capped. surface-sunken sits below the ground and is not on the ramp at all — switch to dark and the shadows stop doing the work.

level 0 · surface — the page ground, and a module's screen
level 1 · surface-raised — cards, list wells, the web sidebar
level 2 · surface-overlay — sheets, dialogs, tooltips. The ramp stops here.
off-ramp · surface-sunken — a grouped-list well, below the ground

Compact is a column-density change

All five compact-by-default screens have interactive rows, so the 44 pt floor binds and compact cannot buy row height. What it buys is columns: one space step off the horizontal padding, the optional secondary line moved out, and divider instead of border. That is what fits a ledger's eight columns without horizontal scroll. Designing it as row-squeezing produces a target-size failure in CI.
